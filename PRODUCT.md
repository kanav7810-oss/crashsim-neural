# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Crash-safety engineers and researchers working through NHTSA-style crash test data. They are mid-analysis, seated at a high-DPI desktop monitor, often in a dim lab or office. Their job: change a vehicle geometry, see instantly how HIC, chest g-force, intrusion, and fatality risk respond, and judge the PINN prediction against the physics engine and a linear FEA baseline.

## Product Purpose

CRASHSIM-NEURAL predicts occupant injury outcomes for a vehicle geometry and crash configuration with a physics-informed neural network, and benchmarks those predictions against a linear FEA baseline. Success is an instrument: trustworthy, dense, fast. Parameter changes give immediate feedback, numbers read like lab readings, and the machine-learning story is told with data, never with marketing.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, expert. Technical and calm, like a research instrument readout. No hype, no decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dark dashboards: navy plus blue accent, stacked rounded cards, big-number-small-label stat tiles, gradient accents, bordered-pill navigation. Anything that reads as a template. Also the "crash equals fire equals orange alarm" cliche. This tool is measurement, not alarm.

## Design Principles

- Earn trust with density and precision; the interface disappears into the task.
- Numbers are the story: tabular figures, mono data, aligned columns, consistent units.
- One accent for actions and selection only; semantic status colors are reserved for risk.
- Structure with hairlines and spacing, not nested cards.
- Restraint in motion: fast, state-driven, invisible when possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on text and controls. Visible focus rings. prefers-reduced-motion respected for animation and transitions.
